## Tapered roller bearings

- [EXTERNAL] Tapered roller bearings use conical rollers that run on conical races, and carry both radial and axial loads. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapered_roller_bearing and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing (both read 2026-11-10)
- [EXTERNAL] The surfaces of the raceways and the roller axes, projected, meet at a common point on the main axis of the bearing. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapered_roller_bearing (read 2026-11-10); Schaeffler, "Tapered roller bearings": the projected lines of contact meet at a point on the bearing axis, https://medias-at.schaeffler.com/en/tapered-roller-bearings
- [EXTERNAL] The taper means there is no sliding motion between the raceways and the outside of the rollers: they roll. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapered_roller_bearing (read 2026-11-10)
- [EXTERNAL] The larger the half angle of the cones, the larger the axial force the bearing can carry. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapered_roller_bearing (read 2026-11-10)
- [EXTERNAL] A radial load on a tapered roller bearing produces an axial component, so a single-row bearing is set against a second one. — Schaeffler, https://medias-at.schaeffler.com/en/tapered-roller-bearings (read 2026-11-10)
- [EXTERNAL] Tapered roller bearings are used, for example, as the wheel bearings of most wheeled land vehicles; pairs of them are used in car wheels, taking the vertical and the sideways forces. — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tapered_roller_bearing (read 2026-11-10)
- [BUILT] The model has fourteen rollers, an inner ring, an outer ring and a cage, as separate closed bodies; its rollers are tilted about 11 degrees from the bearing axis, and their lines meet the axis about 80 units (a little under four outer radii) from the roller, off the picture. — measured from `research/assets/Bearing_with_Tapered_Roller.stl` (principal axis of one roller's profile)
