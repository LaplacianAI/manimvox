# Why is the same page faster the second time?: brief

## Who it is for

Curious people who use the web all day and have never looked behind it; no programming needed. (chosen by the agent)

## What they take away

A cache is a small store of recent answers. The first time, the app server has to ask the slow database and keeps a copy; the second time, it answers from the copy and never touches the database. (chosen by the agent)

## How it should feel

Calm and clear: a small "oh, that is why". (chosen by the agent)

## Your angle

One request followed twice over the same four parts, with the stream of dots going exactly where the voice says: out to the cache and, the first time, on to the database. Marked on screen as one common design, since sites differ. (chosen by the agent)

## How it opens

Straight in, no title card: the four parts on screen and the question typing over them. (chosen by the agent)

## How it ends

Answer the question in the same words, over the same four parts with the database dimmed. (chosen by the agent)

## Tone of voice

Plain spoken English, short sentences, no hype. (chosen by the agent)

## The look

Calm style on a dark slate ground, thin strokes, one blue for requests and one green for answers; no header or progress bar. (chosen by the agent)

## Length and pace

About 45 seconds. (chosen by the agent)
