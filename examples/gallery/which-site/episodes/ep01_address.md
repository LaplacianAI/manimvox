# Script: Which site am I on?

About 80 spoken words; about 38 s at 2.6 words a second plus pauses and the answer. One chapter, no card.

## Opening (cardless, the window is already on screen)
1. "Which part of this window shows which site you are on?" [whole Firefox window; the question types over the band above it] (beat)

## The top strip (cardless chapter; zoom into the top left)
2. "Start at the top." [zoom into tab, buttons, address bar]
3. "The tab shows the page's title, but the page chooses that itself." [leader: the tab]
4. "The address bar is different. It belongs to the browser, so a page cannot write it." [leader: the address bar]
5. "Its domain names the site your browser actually asked for." [leader: the domain]
6. "And the shield says the connection to it is encrypted." [leader: the shield]

## Ending
7. Zoom out. Answer over the question: "The domain in the address bar, and the shield beside it." Narration: "Read the address bar. The domain says which site you are on, and the shield says the connection is encrypted." [window; credit line]

## Critique
1. Problem on screen in 20 s? yes: the real window and the question. 2. One question, answered in its own words? yes. 3. Promise and route: the question; the route is the tour. 4. Terms once, after need: domain, shield. 5. Takeaway: line 7. 6. One chapter, bridge "Start at the top". 7. Same window throughout. 8. Ending on the same window. 9. No recap. 10. Next: not needed at this length. 11. About 80 words, one chapter: yes.
