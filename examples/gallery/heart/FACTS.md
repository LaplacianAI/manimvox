## Where and what

- [SOURCE] The heart is in the center of the chest, near the lungs. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy
- [SOURCE] It has four hollow chambers surrounded by muscle and other heart tissue. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy
- [SOURCE] The two upper chambers are the atria; the two lower chambers are the ventricles. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy; Cleveland Clinic
- [SOURCE] A wall of tissue called the septum divides the right and left sides. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy
- [SOURCE] Heart valves separate the chambers and keep blood flowing in the right direction. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy

## Blood flow

- [SOURCE] Oxygen-poor blood from the body enters through two large veins, the superior and inferior vena cava, into the right atrium. — NHLBI, Blood Flow; Cleveland Clinic
- [SOURCE] The right ventricle pumps blood through the pulmonary valve into the pulmonary artery, which carries it to the lungs. — NHLBI, Blood Flow; Cleveland Clinic
- [SOURCE] Oxygen-rich blood returns through the pulmonary veins to the left atrium. — NHLBI, Blood Flow; Cleveland Clinic
- [SOURCE] The left ventricle pumps blood through the aortic valve into the aorta, a large artery, to the whole body. — NHLBI, Blood Flow; Cleveland Clinic

## Valves

- [SOURCE] The tricuspid valve separates the right atrium and right ventricle. — NHLBI, Blood Flow
- [SOURCE] The mitral valve separates the left atrium and left ventricle. — NHLBI, Blood Flow
- [SOURCE] The pulmonary valve separates the right ventricle and the pulmonary artery. — NHLBI, Blood Flow
- [SOURCE] The aortic valve separates the left ventricle and aorta. — NHLBI, Blood Flow
- [SOURCE] Blood flows from the body and lungs to the atria, and from the atria to the ventricles; the ventricles pump blood out of the heart. — NHLBI, Heart Anatomy

## The model

- [SOURCE] The heart shown is a four-chamber model generated from end-diastolic CT of a patient with no cardiac condition found in follow-up (case 18). — Rodero et al., "Virtual cohort of adult healthy four-chamber heart meshes from CT images", Zenodo, 2021, doi:10.5281/zenodo.4590294, CC BY 4.0; credited on screen
- Its labels (chamber walls, aorta, pulmonary artery, the four valve planes, the vein inlets) come from the dataset; the four-chamber cut runs through the left ventricle's apex and the centres of the mitral and tricuspid valves.
- The aorta and pulmonary artery lie in front of that cut, so the video marks where blood leaves through them with dashed arrows.
