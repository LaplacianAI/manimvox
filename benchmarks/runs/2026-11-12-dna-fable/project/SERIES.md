# How DNA packs into a chromosome — outline

One video, about a minute, workflow: manimvox-concept (a topic with no source document).

## The question

Question: How does two metres of DNA pack into tiny chromosomes?

Situation that shows it: every human cell holds about 2 m of DNA, and its nucleus is about 6 µm across. Shown as a turning double helix with the two numbers under it.

## The viewer

Someone who met DNA and chromosomes at school and remembers the words only. Does not know histone, nucleosome or condensin; each is defined when spoken.

## The theme

Mood from the topic (no brand): a dark microscope-like stage. DNA = `DNA` (blue) and `DNA_TWO` (teal), histones = `HISTONE` (orange), condensin = `CONDENSIN` (pink), accent yellow for the word being said.
