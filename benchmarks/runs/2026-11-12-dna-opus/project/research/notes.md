# Research notes (fetched 2026-11-12)

## Nature Scitable, "DNA Packaging: Nucleosomes and Chromatin" (Annunziato, Nature Education 2008)
https://www.nature.com/scitable/topicpage/dna-packaging-nucleosomes-and-chromatin-310/
- diploid human cell ~6 billion bp; "each diploid cell therefore contains about 2 meters of DNA"; 0.34 nm per bp
- nucleus "typically only 10 to 20 microns in diameter"
- nucleosome: "eight histone proteins and about 146 base pairs of DNA"; two each of H2A, H2B, H3, H4 = octamer; DNA wraps ~1.7 turns (figure text: 1.65)
- linker DNA averages about 20 bp
- nucleosome with DNA ~11 nm (caption: ~10 nm)
- nucleosomes shorten fibre about sevenfold
- "Nucleosomes fold up to form a 30-nanometer chromatin fiber, which forms loops averaging 300 nanometers in length"
- "the packing ratio of DNA in metaphase chromosomes is approximately 10,000:1"

## Wikipedia, Chromatin  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatin
- beads-on-a-string ~10-11 nm under low-salt / reconstituted conditions
- "30-nm chromatin fiber has been observed in vitro, although its presence and prevalence in living cells remain debated."
- cryo-EM: chromatin in many cell types "forms irregular and dynamic nucleosome assemblies rather than regular 30-nm fibers"
- ~147 bp wrap around the histone octamer to form the core particle

## Wikipedia, Nucleosome  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleosome
- ~146 bp (146 and 147 observed in different crystals), 1.67 left-handed superhelical turns around a histone octamer
- octamer: two copies each of H2A, H2B, H3, H4
- cylinder 11 nm diameter, 5.5 nm high

## Paul, Hochwagen & Ercan 2018, "Condensin action and compaction", Curr Genet  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6421088
- "Loop extrusion is likely one of the central drivers of chromosome compaction."
- metazoans: "condensin II mediates longer loops and sets up the axis of mitotic chromosomes"; condensin I shorter loops that thicken them
