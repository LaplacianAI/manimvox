# ep01 — How UPI payments work (script)

Cold open: You scan a code and type a PIN. A second later, the shop has your money.
Intro: How does money cross two banks that fast? By the end, you will follow one payment from your phone to the shop.
Ch1 "Four parties": NPCI built UPI. A payment involves your app, a central switch run by NPCI, and two banks. Payee identified by a UPI ID.
Ch2 "One payment": pick payee, amount, PIN; NPCI looks up payee bank, asks yours; yours checks PIN and balance, debits; shop's bank credits; confirmation returns.
Outro: one switch joins two banks; one request debits and credits. August 2025: about twenty billion payments.
Critique: hook yes; one idea per chapter yes; terms defined yes; answer in outro yes; cut: limits, QR/collect flows, security detail.
